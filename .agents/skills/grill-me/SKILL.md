# Clarify the decision

Identify the smallest unresolved choice that changes the design, consequences
or safety. Offer a short recommendation and concrete options, then let the
maintainer decide. Ask one focused question at a time when practical. Do not
interrogate the user over harmless defaults, and do not block a clear request.
