# Regain direction

Use when the maintainer or agent is uncertain about the goal, priorities, or
next step, or when the work appears to have drifted.

1. **Pause.** Stop implementation; do not silently expand scope or make changes.
2. **Reconstruct.** Inspect the available request, working-tree status, active
   backlog work, acceptance criteria, recent changes, and test results when
   available. Separate observed facts from assumptions.
3. **Summarize.** Briefly state the goal, verified work, open items, drift, and
   unknowns. Say when there is no clear active task or goal.
4. **Offer a path.** Give up to three concrete options, including a small,
   low-risk next action when possible. Recommend one and explain why.
5. **Resume deliberately.** Ask one focused question when a choice is needed;
   after the maintainer chooses, restate the next step and stay within it.

Never discard changes, rewrite plans, close tasks, commit, push, publish or
release without explicit authorization. Missing evidence is not success.
